what_i_learned: |
  Today was the first day of the online portion of the SAIRI Institute. I learned about the goals of the program and was introduced to how artificial intelligence can be used to address real-world problems. We participated in orientation activities, met program staff and fellow participants, and gained an overview of the different research projects that would be completed throughout the institute. I was introduced to the PM2.5 air quality project and began learning about the importance of air pollution research and how satellite and environmental data can be used to study air quality. I also learned about the expectations for the program, including collaboration, research, and professional development.
blockers: |
  Since this was the first day, one challenge was adjusting to the new program environment and becoming familiar with the schedule, technology platforms, and research topics. There was a lot of new information presented, so it took time to understand the program structure and the goals of the project.

reflection: |
 My first day in the SAIRI Institute was both exciting and informative. Although there was a lot to learn, I enjoyed meeting new people and getting an introduction to the research topics we will be exploring throughout the summer. The program already seems like a great opportunity to develop skills in AI, research, and data science. By the end of the day, I felt motivated to learn more about PM2.5 prediction and excited to begin working on a real-world research project with my mentors and teammates.

what_i_learned: |
  Today my group presented our weekly progress and shared the work we have completed on our PM2.5 prediction project. During the presentation, I personally discussed hyperparameters and explained their role in machine learning models. I learned that hyperparameters are settings chosen before training a model that can significantly impact its performance and accuracy. I explained how techniques such as GridSearchCV and RandomizedSearchCV can be used to test different hyperparameter combinations and find the best-performing model. Preparing for the presentation helped me strengthen my understanding of hyperparameters because I had to explain the concept clearly to others. I also learned more about public speaking and how to communicate technical information in a way that is easy for an audience to understand. Listening to my teammates present their sections helped me gain a better understanding of the progress being made across all areas of our project. Overall, today’s presentation reinforced many of the concepts we have been learning throughout the program.

I also wanted to make sure I could answer questions if they came up during or after the presentation.

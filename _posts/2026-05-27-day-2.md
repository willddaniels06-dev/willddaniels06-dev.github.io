what_i_learned: |
 Today I continued learning about the PM2.5 prediction project and gained a deeper understanding of how artificial intelligence can be applied to environmental research. I met with my group members and mentors to discuss our project’s objectives and the types of data we would be using. I learned that PM2.5 refers to very small air pollution particles that can have serious effects on human health when inhaled. We explored how satellite observations and weather measurements can be combined to help estimate air quality levels. I was introduced to the concept of using machine learning models to identify patterns within environmental datasets and make predictions. Throughout the day, I learned more about the importance of data collection and why high-quality data is essential for accurate predictions. I also gained a better understanding of how teamwork plays a major role in research projects and how different team members contribute unique ideas and skills. During discussions, I learned more about the steps involved in conducting scientific research, from gathering data to analyzing results. I was introduced to additional tools and resources that we would use throughout the program to support our work. By the end of the day, I had a clearer picture of how our project would develop over the coming weeks and the skills I would need to build along the way.

Managing all the new concepts while keeping track of project expectations required focus and patience. Despite these challenges, I continued asking questions and participating in discussions to improve my understanding.
